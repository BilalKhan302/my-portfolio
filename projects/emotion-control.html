Parameter-Efficient Emotion Control

A state-of-the-art framework for controlling emotional intensity in image generation using Dynamic LoRa and VA values.

Problem Statement

Traditional emotion control in AI generation relies on discrete labels (e.g., "happy", "sad"), which fails to capture the continuous nature of human affect. Furthermore, full model fine-tuning for specific emotions is computationally expensive and rigid.

Methodology & Approach

This research introduces a Parameter-Efficient Framework utilizing Dynamic LoRa modules. Instead of fixed categories, we map emotions onto the Valence-Arousal (VA) space, allowing for nuanced intensity control. By implementing a Base + Delta training option, we achieved significant parameter reduction while maintaining high fidelity in subtle expressions.

Key Features

  • Image-to-Image transfer with precise emotion mapping.
  • Surpassing SOTA (State Of The Art) results in affect-intensity evaluations.
  • Optimized for parameter reduction without quality loss.
